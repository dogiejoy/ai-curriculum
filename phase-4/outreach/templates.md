## Follow-up Sequence (per contact)

**Day 0**: Initial email (Template A/B/C based on target)
**Day 5**: Follow-up
> Hi [Name] — checking my earlier note re: [1-phrase summary]. Understand if the timing isn't right. Anything I can do to help make it easier to evaluate? Happy to send more specific info if you tell me your use case.

**Day 12**: Final touch (soft close)
> Last note from me — moving on if there's no fit. If you know someone else building on Laravel who might need this: intro appreciated. Either way, best of luck with [Product/Project].

**Then STOP**. No more emails. Real prospects will remember; forcing more emails hurts brand.

## Personalization Checklist

Before sending, verify:
- [ ] Recipient name (correct spelling)
- [ ] Company name (correct)
- [ ] Specific reference to their work (product/launch/hiring/blog post)
- [ ] Subject line matches template (not generic)
- [ ] Link works (case study loads)
- [ ] Response signature includes at least 1 contact method

---

## Metrics to Track (per week)

Simple spreadsheet:
- Prospect name + company
- Channel (email/LinkedIn/other)
- Sent date
- Reply date (or blank)
- Reply category (interested / not-fit / no-response / hostile)
- Call scheduled (Y/N)
- Notes

Target: 20-30 sent per week, 2-4 replies, 1-2 calls scheduled.

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Don't attach PDFs or slide decks in cold outreach
- Don't send from noreply@ or generic addresses  
- Don't include "Sent from my iPhone" (looks unprofessional even if true)
- Don't ask for "15 minutes of their time" (feels dishonest — meetings are 30 min minimum)
- Don't say "I saw you on LinkedIn" (obviously — everyone knows)
- Don't apologize for the cold outreach — you're offering value
- Don't send Monday morning or Friday afternoon (timing = mid-week midday best)
